The Blessing Button

Might, Wisdom or Kings, for whoever's next. One press at a time.

One button. Everybody blessed.

A small button sits on your Paladin bar. Each press casts your Blessing on the next friendly player who's missing one from you, nearest first, and moves straight on to the next. Keep pressing until the whole group is glowing.

Who's next, in order

  1. You, with the Blessing that fits your spec: Might for Retribution and Protection, Wisdom for Holy.
  2. Your group, nearest first when the game reports distance.
  3. Your target, then friendly players with a visible nameplate.

Class-smart

  • Blessing of Might for Warriors, Rogues and Hunters.
  • Blessing of Wisdom for casters and healers.
  • Kings for everyone, if you switch it on (once you know Blessing of Kings). Every class can be overridden under Settings › Paladin bar.
  • Greater Blessings only when you know them, in a raid, when three or more of a class need one.

It skips

  • anyone who already has your Blessing
  • anyone out of range (the tooltip tells you how many)
  • anyone wearing your Blessing of Freedom or Protection, until it ends: one Blessing from you per target, and Might would replace it. Then they're offered again.

Blizzard's rules, kept

  • One press, one cast. The addon never casts on its own, and it adds no macros to your macro list.
  • Who the button targets only changes out of combat. In combat it keeps its last target and says so.
  • No key is bound by default. Pick one under Settings › Paladin bar › Set key or type /tristan bless key. Super combos are never used.

    PvP bar

    Five secure buttons, one press one cast:

    • Freedom on your mouseover, focus, picked ally, else you
    • Cleanse (Purify until you have Cleanse)
    • Protection on mouseover, picked ally or focus
    • Stun: Hammer of Justice on your target, Shift for focus
    • Repentance, same as Stun (talent)

    My cooldowns & Forbearance

    Your own cooldowns (Hammer of Justice, Divine Shield, Blessing of Protection, Lay on Hands and friends) and your Forbearance, so you know which "get out of jail" card is still in your hand.

    Burst coach

    Walks you through a burst window step by step, following only your own casts. Advice only: it never presses anything, and it never reads your enemy.

    World PvP

    Is this zone contested or hostile, are you flagged, what's ready if you get jumped. Set for a PvP realm by default, with tips for leveling on one. A Buff before the gates open reminder waits in battlegrounds.

    What it can't do (and won't pretend to)

    • No enemy trinket or cooldown tracking
    • No diminishing-returns tracker
    • No kill announcer and no battleground callouts
    • No Spy-style enemy detection
    • No auto-targeting enemy healers

    Why: Forever gives addons no combat log, hides other units' values as secrets, and locks chat in instances. For nameplates, try BetterBlizzPlates or Plater; for battleground timers, Capping.

      The Tavern

      Questions, answered by the innkeeper.

      Innkeeper Emberwick

      Well met, Paladin! Mind the hammer on the table. What would you like to know?

      Does it work on Classic Era or retail?

      No. It's built for World of Warcraft: Forever only, and other clients won't load it. Forever's spells, talents, battlegrounds and gear all differ from Classic.

      Why doesn't it track enemy trinkets, cooldowns or DR?

      Because it can't. Forever gives addons no combat log, hides other units' values as secrets, and locks chat in instances. So no enemy trackers, no DR, no kill announcer, no callouts, no Spy-style detection and no auto-targeting healers. For nameplates try BetterBlizzPlates or Plater; for battleground timers, Capping.

      Is this automation?

      No. One press is one cast. The Blessing button and the PvP bar are secure buttons that only cast when you click them or press their key. The rotation helper, the Seal and Aura hints and the Burst coach only advise. You press the buttons.

      Why don't the buttons have keys?

      Keys are optional and none is bound by default, so it never steals a key you already use. Pick one under Settings › Paladin bar. Super (Meta) combos are never offered: on Omarchy and many Linux desktops they belong to the desktop.

      Is Battle a separate addon?

      No. Battle is part of the same window, one group in the sidebar next to Rotation, Gear and the rest. Type /tristan battle to jump straight there.

      What does it cost?

      Nothing. It's free and open source under the MIT license.

      Is there one for other classes?

      Yes, from the same workshop: Andric's Addon for Priests and Parzelle's Addon for Mages. And My Azeroth Life is where the whole adventure lives.

      Is this made by Blizzard?

      No. Tristan's Addon is an unofficial, free and open source fan project by Nine Point Labs. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment.
